## Full description (max 4000 characters)
Two answers were written by bots. One was written by the person sitting next to you. Can you tell which one is human?

Human or AI? is a quick pass-and-play party game for two players on one phone.

HOW IT WORKS
- You get a question, like "What did you want to be when you were 8?"
- You type your answer, then pass the phone.
- Your opponent sees three answers: yours and two written by bots.
- They pick the one they think is human.

HOW TO WIN
- Guessers score by finding the human.
- Writers score by hiding among the bots.
- Sound like a bot, or say something nobody expects from you. Your call.

WHY YOU'LL LIKE IT
- 60 funny questions about food, dreams, fears, childhood and more
- Rounds take under a minute
- Works offline. No account, no ads, no data collected
- Great for road trips, dinner tables and waiting rooms

Play 2, 3 or 5 rounds each, then demand a rematch.
