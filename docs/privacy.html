Human or AI? Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Human or AI? is a pass-and-play party game made by Aliakbar Mehdizadeh. This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app does not collect, store, send or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What you type

Player names and answers you type stay on your phone during the game and are cleared when the game ends. They are never sent anywhere. The app does not request internet access.

Bot answers

The bot answers in the game were written in advance and are built into the app. The app does not connect to any AI service while you play.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version collects any data, for example for online matches, this page will be updated before that version is released.
